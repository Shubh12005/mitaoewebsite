Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Find prompt answers to the most common queries asked by engineering aspirants and parents.

MITAOE offers 4-year undergraduate B.Tech programs in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML), Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science), Computer Engineering (Software Engineering), Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence.
Admissions are conducted primarily through the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET) and Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main Paper-1), as per the eligibility criteria set by the State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra.
All undergraduate Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs at MITAOE have a duration of 4 academic years divided into 8 semesters, including comprehensive projects and internship periods.
Candidates should register for the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) on the official Maharashtra State CET Cell portal. During option form filling, choose MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi (College Code: 06146). You can also submit an enquiry on this portal for direct guidance and counseling.
Yes, MIT Academy of Engineering is an autonomous institute affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) and accredited with an 'A' grade by NAAC. Autonomy provides academic freedom to design industry-relevant syllabi and modern assessment methodologies.
Yes, internships are an integral component of the academic curriculum. Over 700+ internship opportunities are facilitated through corporate tie-ups, research incubators, and industry capstone semesters.
Yes, MITAOE has an active Training and Placement Cell that hosts 450+ recruiting organizations annually. The highest package recorded stands at ₹28 LPA, with an average package of ₹6.1 LPA across branches.
Yes, safe, hygienic, and well-maintained hostel facilities are available for outstation boys and girls near the campus with Wi-Fi, dining facilities, 24/7 security, and recreational amenities.
MIT Academy of Engineering is located on Alandi Road, Alandi (Devachi), Pune – 412 105, Maharashtra, India. The campus is well connected to Pune Airport, Pune Railway Station, and the PCMC tech corridor.
You can contact the MITAOE Admission Cell directly by phone at +91-9071123436, +91-8722100030, or 020-30253500, or via email at admissions@mitaoe.ac.in. You can also visit our campus admission desk on all working days.
